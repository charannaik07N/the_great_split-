## Design Context

### Users

Cinema buffs on desktop who want to discover new movies quickly.

### Brand Personality

Premium, cinematic, refined.

### Aesthetic Direction

Netflix-inspired, premium editorial feel. Support both light and dark themes while avoiding a purely dark presentation.

### Design Principles

- Lead with cinematic storytelling and premium polish.
- Prioritize fast discovery with clear, scan-friendly hierarchy.
- Use restrained, high-contrast palettes that remain WCAG AA compliant.
- Keep layouts spacious and editorial rather than dense grids.
- Apply motion sparingly and respect reduced-motion preferences.
